    About

    Radius rings are measured on the WGS84 ellipsoid. A parcel is listed when any part of it touches or falls within the ring. Values marked * are total market or land + improvement values where the assessor does not publish a taxable value; acreage marked † is computed from the parcel polygon. Owners marked § are the grantee on the latest recorded deed because the source withholds (or left blank) the taxpayer name; names marked ‡ carry a source caveat shown on hover; sales and prices marked ~ were not treated as arm’s-length market sales by the assessor. Zoning is read from the jurisdiction’s zoning-district layer at the parcel’s label point. Verify with the county assessor before relying on any figure.
